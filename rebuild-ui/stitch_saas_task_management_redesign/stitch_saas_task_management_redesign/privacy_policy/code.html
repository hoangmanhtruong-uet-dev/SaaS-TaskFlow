Section 01

Information We Collect

To deliver high-velocity workflow orchestration, TaskFlow collects specific categories of telemetry, identity, and content data when you interact with our platform.

person_pin Identity & Account Data

Names, corporate email addresses, encrypted passwords, avatar links, and authentication tokens via OAuth providers (GitHub, Google, SSO).

terminal Workflow & Content Data

Task titles, descriptions, comments, attachments, sprint boards, labels, custom attributes, and webhook configurations created within workspaces.

analytics Telemetry & Device Logs

IP addresses, browser fingerprints, operating system versions, feature interaction metrics, and API request latency logs.

payments Billing Metadata

Stripe-processed tokenized payment instruments, billing addresses, invoice history, and seat license counts. We never store raw credit card numbers.

Section 03

Data Retention & Lifecycle

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or to comply with legal, tax, or accounting requirements.

30 Days Soft Delete Window

Deleted tasks and workspaces remain in the trash bin for 30 days before permanent cryptographic shredding.

90 Days Audit & Security Logs

IP access logs, rate-limit violations, and failed authentication attempts automatically purge after 90 days.

7 Years Financial Records

Tax invoices, subscription receipts, and billing identifiers are retained in compliance with standard corporate law.

Section 04

Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area or California), you possess robust statutory rights regarding your personal data.

visibility Right to Access

Request a machine-readable JSON export of all profile attributes, workspace memberships, and task histories associated with your account.

edit Right to Rectification

Instantly update or correct inaccurate personal details directly within your account settings or via our support desk.

delete_forever Right to Erasure ("To Be Forgotten")

Permanently wipe your personal account and disassociate your contributions from public or shared team workspaces.

block Right to Restrict Processing

Opt-out of telemetry tracking, marketing communications, and automated profiling without affecting core task features.
