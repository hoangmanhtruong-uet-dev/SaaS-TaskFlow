Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about TaskFlow subscriptions and features.

How does seat-based pricing work when adding team members mid-billing cycle? expand_more

TaskFlow uses prorated billing. When you invite a new team member, you are only charged for the remaining days left in your current billing cycle. The exact prorated amount is calculated automatically at checkout.

Can I migrate my issues and tasks from Jira or Linear? expand_more

Yes! We provide one-click importers for Jira, Linear, Asana, and GitHub Issues. Navigate to Workspace Settings > Import & Export to start your migration securely in under 5 minutes.

What security standards and compliance certifications does TaskFlow meet? expand_more

TaskFlow is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and HIPAA ready for Enterprise tier customers. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).

How do I contact support if I encounter a bug? expand_more

You can reach our engineering support team 24/7 by clicking the "Contact Support" button below or using the in-app command palette (`⌘K`) and typing "Send feedback to support".
